The Emergency Dilemma

In a stressful moment,
seconds and clarity matter.

When an unexpected crisis occurs, cognitive overwhelm and adrenaline make ordinary smartphone interactions slow, fragmented, and prone to error.

Critical Seconds Lost

Unlocking a phone, searching through hundreds of contacts, and manually drafting text messages while in distress burns crucial response time.

  • Manual typing under adrenaline stress
  • Scattered emergency phone numbers
  • Delay in alerting multiple trusted guardians

Inability to Share Accurate Coordinates

Explaining an exact physical street corner, landmark, or GPS coordinate while panicking is often impossible, confusing first responders and family.

  • Vague or inaccurate verbal location descriptions
  • Complex manual map pin sharing steps
  • No automated continuous location trail

Invasive Tracking vs Real Privacy

Many commercial tracking apps constantly track users in the background, draining battery and violating personal privacy when no emergency exists.

  • Persistent, invasive background location monitoring
  • Third-party data harvesting and ad targeting
  • Ambiguous permissions that deter user trust

Privacy & Data Ethics

Protection without intrusion:
safety with zero compromise.

Emergency applications require sensitive permissions. RakshaX is architected on a strict "SOS-Only" principle, ensuring your personal coordinates remain completely private during daily life.

Location strictly during SOS

GPS coordinates are polled only at the exact instant an emergency SOS is dispatched. There is zero background tracking or location polling during ordinary day-to-day device use.

ACCESS_FINE_LOCATIONEngaged only upon active SOS trigger

Targeted Emergency SMS

Telephony access is scoped strictly to dispatching distress alerts to user-designated trusted contacts. No marketing messages, unverified broadcasts, or third-party gateways.

SEND_SMSDirect operator SMS to saved guardians

Local Contact Circle

Contacts permission is requested exclusively to allow users to select trusted family members and guardians from their phonebook. Contact lists are never exported to commercial servers.

READ_CONTACTSLocal device selection & storage

Controlled 15-20 Min Cycle

During an ongoing crisis, automated background emails update saved contacts every 15-20 minutes. As soon as the user confirms safety, all background polling immediately terminates.

BACKGROUND_JOBTerminated immediately when SOS cleared

Zero Commercial Advertising or Third-Party Data Selling

RakshaX does not monetize user distress. The project is created as a social innovation contribution for the Seva First Innovation Challenge, strictly prioritizing human life, transparent permissions, and reliable mobile safety.
